# Stuiver

A free, simple budget tracker built **by a student in the Netherlands, for students in the Netherlands** who want to keep an eye on their money.

*Stuiver* is an old Dutch coin worth five cents, a small amount that adds up.

## Why it exists

Student life in the Netherlands comes with its own money questions: rent (huur), health insurance (zorgverzekering), DUO, rent and healthcare allowances (toeslagen), a deposit (borg), and lots of small shop visits (Dirk, Albert Heijn, Lidl, GVB). The app started as a personal Excel sheet of a student's first months in Amsterdam and grew into an app, designed together with Claude (an AI assistant by Anthropic).

## Features

- Income and expenses with your own categories (rename, add, mark some as "outside the budget", e.g. deposit and start-up costs)
- Week, month and year views with a balance chart and category breakdown
- Monthly budgets per category, warnings at 80% and 100%, and an end-of-month forecast with a daily spending limit
- Recurring entries (rent, insurance, DUO, subscriptions) added automatically each month
- Quick add: suggestions of your recent shops and categories
- Rate how useful each purchase was (1 to 5) and review unrated ones one by one
- Search, per-shop totals, month-to-month comparison with percentage change
- Display in EUR or PLN, using the rate from the day of each transaction
- Yearly statistics: monthly averages, top shops, a chart by category
- Light and dark theme, works offline, installable on your phone's home screen
- Backup to a file and restore from it

## Privacy

There is no account and no server. All entries are stored **only on your device** (in the browser's local storage). Make a backup from Settings > General > "Pobierz kopię zapasową" (download backup), especially before clearing browser data or uninstalling.

## Install on your phone

Open the site address in your phone's browser, then:

- **Android (Chrome):** menu (three dots) > "Install app"
- **iPhone (Safari):** Share > "Add to Home Screen"
